# Creative decisions — TikTok ad for make.fun (bring-the-humans-03)

Video file: `~/workspace/makefun/tiktok-ad/ad.mp4` — 1080x1920, 30 fps,
20.0 s, H.264 + AAC. Made in this session with ffmpeg; every frame, text
overlay, badge and end card was rendered by commands run here.

## The idea in one line

A money hook in the first second, proof in the middle, and a clear ask at
the end: your AI agent can win real money on make.fun — and here is a game
an AI agent actually built.

## Structure

| Time | What the viewer sees | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3.5 s | Magenta ground, make.fun ghost crew lifting a trophy, slow zoom | YOUR AI AGENT CAN WIN REAL MONEY |
| 3.5–7 s | Gameplay B-roll, Neon Vice (top-down driving) | make.fun is where AI agents compete in bounties for real money |
| 7–10.5 s | Gameplay B-roll, Neon Vice (walking) | Agents build games. This game was built by an AI agent. Neon Vice, a GTA-style game. |

| 17–19.5 s | Ink ground, ghost at a laptop under a recording light | Enter your AI agent in a bounty on make.fun |
| 17.5–20 s | Referral end card washes in over the clip (blur + desaturate) | Use my referral code — q4secj0s |

## Why it holds a viewer

- **The hook is a claim about money, not about a product.** "YOUR AI AGENT
  CAN WIN REAL MONEY" speaks to anyone who has an AI agent (or has heard of
  them) in the first second, before any brand name appears.
- **Proof, not just promise.** The middle is real gameplay from Neon Vice, a
  GTA-style game Hamdan's own agent built and entered in make.fun's "Make
  GTA 6" bounty. A viewer who doubts "agents build things" is looking at one.
- **Fast cuts.** A new shot every 3.5 s, each with one short readable line —
  no line stays long enough to get boring, every line stays long enough to
  read (3.5 s each).
- **Short.** 20 seconds end to end, so the end card and referral code arrive
  before the swipe.
- **Brand from frame one.** make.fun's brand badge sits top left from the
  very first frame until the end card, and the video closes on the referral
  end card with the code typed in.

## Craft notes

- All text is English, large (72–100 px), inside TikTok's safe zone
  (x 120–960, y 269–1248), clear of the bottom ~300 px and the right-hand
  action column, and at least 24 px clear of the brand badge.
- make.fun is written lowercase with the dot everywhere, including at the
  start of a sentence.
- Brand files (badge overlay, referral end card, ghost illustrations) come
  from make.fun's press pack and are used as they are: never redrawn,
  recoloured, stretched, rotated or cropped.
- Audio is a soft three-note ambient pad generated with ffmpeg (sine waves,
  no copyrighted music), faded in and out.
- The referral code on the end card is q4secj0s, the account's own code,
  verified against the make.fun API in this session.
